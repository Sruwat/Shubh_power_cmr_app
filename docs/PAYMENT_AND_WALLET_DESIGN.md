# Payment And Wallet Design

## Implemented

- Mock payment intent.
- Demo payment completion.
- Wallet top-up.
- Wallet balance derived from immutable ledger entries.
- Invoice and refund placeholder endpoints.
- MongoDB persistence when Atlas is configured.
- Idempotency-key support for payment intents and wallet top-ups.

## Future

- Razorpay provider adapter.
- UPI/card tokenization handled by payment provider.
- GST-compliant invoice generation.
- Refund reconciliation.
