# Shubh Power Design System

Implemented in `mobile/src/design-system`.

## Tokens

- Colors: primary, secondary, navy, ink, body, background, surface, border, success, warning, error, unknown.
- Type: display, h1, h2, h3, body, caption and tab sizes.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 18 and 24.
- Radius: 14 controls, 16 buttons, 20 cards, 24 panels, pill 999.

## Components

- Screen
- Card
- Title
- Body
- Badge
- PrimaryButton
- SecondaryButton
- Field
- StationCard

## States Covered

- Loading through TanStack Query.
- Empty station lists through list rendering.
- Permission denied through onboarding copy and manual fallback.
- Research station badge.
- Demo charging enabled badge.
- Active charging card and stop action.
